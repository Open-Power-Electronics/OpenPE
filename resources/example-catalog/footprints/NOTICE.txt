KiCad library subset for the nine curated reference examples.
Official KiCad 10 models are copied unchanged from the pinned bundled library.
Missing models are copied unchanged from the pinned source checkout under res/kicad/3dmodels.
The WE-DD-7345straight footprint is extracted from the hash-pinned LM25017 expert PCB; board-instance links are removed and its model uses a project-relative reference.
See manifest.json for SHA-256 hashes and sources, ../manifest.json for the source PCB hash, LICENSE.md and CREDITS.md for upstream terms and attribution.
