OpenPE example-controller datasheet references

The downloaded PDFs under examples/*/datasheets/ are original Texas Instruments
and STMicroelectronics datasheets obtained from the official HTTPS URLs recorded
in the bundled internal reference catalog. The Lite-On LTV-817S PDF is the
manufacturer-authored document obtained from the explicitly pinned Hitech
distributor copy in the supplemental EMC file catalog.
They retain all embedded vendor copyright, trademark, legal and product notices.
No Apache-2.0 license or other redistribution grant is asserted for those PDFs.
The PDFs are excluded from Git and desktop packages.
Retain the original PDF notices when keeping local reference copies.

The JSON records contain selected numerical specifications, conditions,
document identities and source locators reviewed against those original PDFs.
They do not replace the complete datasheets and do not establish board-level
performance, simulation equivalence or hardware qualification.
