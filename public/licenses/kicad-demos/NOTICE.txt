OpenPE includes unchanged upstream schematics and a generated offline snapshot.
The derived OpenPE example documents and native exports retain this license.
Changes: conversion to OpenPE JSON, fresh instance UUIDs and display titles;
instance field positions, visibility and reading direction arranged for clarity;
exports normalize the KiCad 10 header and property visibility syntax;
active-sheet instance references take precedence over stale property text.
Embedded symbols, connections, custom properties and schematic text are retained.
The Buck IRF540N VDMOS model is also bundled as an unchanged model definition
extracted from IRF-Power-VDMOS.mod, with generated electrical pin signatures.
Original model SHA-256: 840295ba890baad25b3921fb9ab17f6174cf9673f58fde37f26bc3ca1cdde596
Other external model files, full KiCad project settings and PCB are not included.
