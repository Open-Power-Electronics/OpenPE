OpenPE example simulation models

The nine retained simulation schematics are from KiCad 10.0.0, commit
0feeca2a807f428ad2b3fa7c1e39625cb769f02c, KiCad demo contributors.
See kicad-demos/NOTICE and LICENSE for schematic attribution.

Except for the separately documented bundled Buck model, external example
models are downloaded during explicit example preparation from the commit-pinned official KiCad source
mirror or KiCad symbol repository. OpenPE verifies their SHA-256 and stores
unchanged files, including upstream notices, in its private application cache.
Vendor model bytes are not included in the OpenPE repository or application.
Model providers retain their respective copyrights, terms and limitations.
The model's own notices govern its use; the schematic license does not grant
additional rights over third-party models. No vendor endorsement is implied.

Model sources: resources/example-models-lock.json in the OpenPE source release.
https://github.com/KiCad/kicad-source-mirror/tree/0feeca2a807f428ad2b3fa7c1e39625cb769f02c/demos/simulation
https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries/kicad-symbols

Included model families: KiCad demo PWM and Class-D driver; generic opamps
(Holger Vogt, Public Domain); LM317/rectifier/transformer demo models;
BC337-25, 1N5818, AD8009, FZT1049A and laser diode demo models;
GaN Systems GS-065-018-2-L and STMicroelectronics STPSC5H12D.

Models describe approximations under their stated limits. Completed transient
results are not ERC, DRC, thermal/loss analysis or production verification.
